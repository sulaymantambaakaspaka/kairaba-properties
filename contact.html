Before You Enquire

Frequently asked

Do you verify land titles?

Always. Before any plot or property changes hands we confirm ownership, check the title at the lands office, and walk you through the paperwork so you know exactly what you are buying.

How does property management work?

We source and vet tenants, collect the rent, run regular inspections, and handle repairs, then send you a monthly statement. The fee is about 8% of the monthly rent.

Do you help diaspora buyers?

Yes. A large part of our work is helping Gambians abroad buy land and homes safely. We send photos and videos, verify titles, and can represent you through to completion.

Can I view a property before I commit?

Of course. Send us the listing or your search and we will arrange a viewing at a time that suits you, in person or over a video call if you are overseas.
