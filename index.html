Why Kairaba

A safe pair of hands for the biggest decision you will make

Kairaba Properties is a family-run agency on Kairaba Avenue in Fajara, led by Modou Sowe. For over fifteen years we have helped Gambian families, returning diaspora buyers, and overseas landlords buy, rent, and manage property along the coast.

Buying land or a home is the biggest decision most families make, so we do it the transparent way. Every title is checked, every valuation is honest, and every step of the paperwork is explained before you sign.

  • Verified titles and due diligence on every plot and property
  • Honest, market-based valuations, never inflated
  • Full management for landlords living abroad
